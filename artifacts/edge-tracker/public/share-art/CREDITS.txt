Share-card artwork («Олимп» theme) — credits and licences
==========================================================

Every image here is a public-domain artwork from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to 4:5 and resized to 1080x1350 WebP
(thumb/ = 160x200 picker previews of the same crops). No photo of a 3-D object, no modern photo with its own licence.
Used as share-card backgrounds in artifacts/edge-tracker/src/components/trades/share-card/themes.ts (ids = file names).
